# Privacy

Updated 4 November 2026.

## Website analytics

This blog uses Google Analytics to understand visits and interactions. Reports include page views, referral sources, approximate city and country, browser and device categories, link clicks, article selections, scrolling, and downloads.

Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to measure activity. Location is estimated from the visitor’s IP address; Google states that Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses. City estimates may be inaccurate.

Analytics does not request your name, email address, or precise location. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled. Reports are available through the site owner’s private dashboard.
